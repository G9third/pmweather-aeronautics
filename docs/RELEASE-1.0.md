## PMAero 1.0

- Adds Immersive Vehicles support through the PMIV addon.
- Adds a shared live wind HUD and four-minute weather test under `/pmaero`.
- Improves tornado updraft and downdraft effects.
- Allows strong wind to overcome wheel and track grip while preserving normal handling below the breakaway threshold.
- Corrects unpowered Offroad wheel rotation during sideways skidding.
- Adds wind drift for PMIV custom particles.
- Prevents duplicate Sable wind forces without changing PMWeather settings.
- Sets body wind influence to 0.1 by default.
